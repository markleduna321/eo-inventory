How to Use QR Codes

View System Details

Scan the QR code with any QR scanner app or camera to view complete system unit details including specifications, status, and assignment information.

Print Labels

Right-click on any QR code image and save/print to create physical labels for your system units. These can be attached to equipment for easy identification.

In-App Scanning

Use the "Scan QR" button in the system units management page to quickly scan and view system details without leaving the application.

Mobile Access

QR codes work on any device - phones, tablets, or desktops. Perfect for field technicians and remote access to system information.
